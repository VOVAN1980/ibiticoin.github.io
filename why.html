FAQ — honest answers, human language, no fairy tales

1) “Is this a scam?” How do I check it properly?

Not with words — with links and the blockchain. Here’s the same basic checklist we would use ourselves:

  • Open the token contract on BscScan and confirm it exists and is verified.
  • Open the IBITI/USDT pool on PancakeSwap and check liquidity and volumes.
  • Look at the pair chart on DexScreener — it shows what’s really happening.
  • Use links only from our website and official social pages.

We never ask for seed phrases, private keys, and we never “help” you in private messages.

2) “Why should I trust you at all?”

Trust is not “believe us”, it’s “verify us”. We build the project so anyone can check everything themselves: contracts, addresses, the pool, and team actions. We build publicly, step by step, without fog or “secret insiders”.

3) “Why is liquidity small? That’s risky.”

Yes — it is a risk. Small liquidity means higher volatility and slippage. We don’t hide that. The project is early, we’re just reaching people and growing the base. Our goal is to increase liquidity calmly and responsibly, not fake numbers for a pretty screenshot.

4) “How are you different from meme coins?”

We don’t start with “moon” or “100x”. Our plan is boring — and that’s why it’s realistic: base → liquidity → useful modules → partnerships. We want the token to be part of an ecosystem (staking / NFT collection), not just a picture.

5) “Do you promise price growth?”

No. We’re developers, not fortune tellers. Price depends on the market, liquidity, demand, and how well the product and community are built. What we can promise is work, transparency, and actual releases.

6) “What is already working right now?”

The token on BNB Chain is live, the IBITI/USDT pool is active, everything is verifiable on BscScan and DexScreener. The website and social channels are live. The website promo mechanism (bonus + referral logic) is active. Staking is open. Next steps: liquidity growth and module releases.

7) “What modules are you building and why?”

We build in modules on purpose — so functionality can grow step by step, without chaos:

  • Staking — incentives for holders and long-term alignment, not only trading.
  • NFT collection — 11 cards from our book, bought with USDT and yours for good.
8) “How does the promo work: +10% and referrals?”

Short and clear:

  • Purchase through the official website mechanism → get +10% IBITI bonus.
  • After your first purchase, the system gives you a personal referral link.
  • A friend makes their first purchase via your link → you receive +1 IBITI to your wallet.

If you buy directly on a DEX without the website, the bonus won’t apply — the site can’t reliably attribute it.

9) “Why BNB Chain?”

Because it’s practical: fast transactions, solid infrastructure, PancakeSwap, and low friction for users. At an early stage, ease of entry really matters.

10) “What risks do you openly acknowledge?”

We don’t pretend there are no risks. The main ones right now:

  • Early stage → low liquidity → volatility.
  • Community and visibility need time to grow.
  • Module development requires testing and careful releases.
  • Scam accounts may impersonate “admins” — only official links are safe.
11) “What do you actually need right now?”

Three things: liquidity, community, and partnerships / integrations. If you’re a developer, marketer, community builder, or simply want to help the project grow — reach out via official channels. We genuinely reply.

12) “Why don’t you DM people first?”

Because it’s safer for everyone. Projects that DM first are a classic scam pattern. We communicate publicly and via the official website. If someone messages you first claiming to be us — treat it as a red flag.
