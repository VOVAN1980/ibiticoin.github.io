📅 Terms and rewards

🎁 NFTs

Staking pays in IBITI only and does not issue NFTs.

Cards from the NFT Gallery are bought with USDT and have no expiry. NFTs are collectibles: they give no discount, no income and no share in the project.

⚠ Early exit

  • 🔻 The penalty is taken from your stake, not only from rewards.
  • 📏 The penalty is the same on the first day and on the last day of the term.
  • ❌ No reward.

Example: 1,000 IBITI for 12 months. Early exit returns 700 IBITI, on time you receive 1,300 IBITI.

⏳ After the term ends

You have 30 days to withdraw your stake with the reward, or to renew.

After those 30 days the reward is lost. Your stake stays yours with no time limit, and you can withdraw it whenever you want.

🔁 Renewing

  • Choose a new amount and a new term. Within the 30 days your reward is yours and counts towards the new stake.
  • If the new amount is lower, the difference goes back to your wallet. If it is higher, you add the difference from your wallet.
  • After the 30 days only your stake counts.
  • A renewal needs enough reward pool for the new reward. You can always withdraw instead.

🏦 Reward pool

  • Rewards are paid from a pool on the staking contract. Your reward is reserved when you stake, so other people's stakes never pay it.
  • When the pool runs out, new stakes are refused. Existing stakes are not affected.
  • A pause stops new stakes and renewals. Withdrawing always stays open.
  • Penalties and lost rewards go back to the project. The project cannot touch your stake or your reserved reward.

📌 Tips

  • Avoid early exit: you lose the reward and part of your stake.
  • Set a reminder for the end of your term, and keep a little BNB in your wallet for gas.
