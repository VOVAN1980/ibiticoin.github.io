Executive Summary

IBITIcoin is an infrastructure project on BNB Smart Chain built around the IBITI token and separate utility modules. The token is deliberately minimal: fixed supply, no owner, no fees. Everything else lives in separate contracts that use the token, while the token knows nothing about them.

Live on mainnet: the IBITI token, team vesting and the website sale. Deployed next, one by one: staking, NFTs and the sell contract with the smart fee. Each one gets its address here once it is deployed and verified.

1) Goals & Design Principles

For normal users
  • Buy IBITI on the website at a fixed price, with a bonus and a referral reward.
  • Trade on PancakeSwap once the pool has liquidity.
  • Stake IBITI and use NFTs once those modules are deployed.
  • Everything visible on-chain (no “trust me, bro”).
For reviewers (BSC / listing)
  • Clear contracts and addresses, with verified source code.
  • Explicit money flow (USDT routing, inventory funding).
  • No proxies: live contracts cannot be upgraded.
  • Risks stated plainly (liquidity, admin power, no independent audit yet).
Why the two-buy model? Market swaps + Website sale

PancakeSwap is the open market. The website sale is a separate on-chain contract for promotions and referrals: it does not replace the market, it provides a fixed-price path with a bonus, strict limits and on-chain rules.

Why is the token so small? Modules live outside it

A token contract cannot be changed after deployment, and scanners judge a token by its own code. Keeping staking, NFTs and fees in separate contracts means each of them can be fixed or replaced by a new deployment, while the token stays the same.

3) IBITI Token (What it is)

Core properties
  • Network: BNB Smart Chain
  • Decimals: 8
  • Total supply: 100,000,000 IBITI, fixed at deployment
  • Token address: 0x71b5...381A
What the token is for
  • Medium of value inside IBITI ecosystem modules
  • Staking (once deployed)

Technical details (reviewer view)
  • Standard ERC-20 with burn, built on OpenZeppelin Contracts 4.9.3 and compiled with solc 0.8.22.
  • The token has no owner, minting, pause, fees, blacklist, proxy or upgrade path.
  • Holders can burn their own tokens, or tokens within an allowance they were given.
  • USDT on BSC has 18 decimals and IBITI has 8, so every price in the system converts between them explicitly.

3.1 Tokenomics & Supply Management

IBITI has a fixed supply of 100,000,000 IBITI (8 decimals) on BNB Smart Chain. The whole supply was created once, in the constructor, and sent straight to its recipients. The token contract itself holds nothing and cannot create more.

Total Supply
  • Total supply: 100,000,000 IBITI
  • Decimals: 8
  • Token address: 0x71b552D3503fe20610aaA470A96E1838fae8381A
Where tokens are held (on-chain)
  • On the wallets and the vesting contract listed in section 3.2.
  • They move by ordinary transfers, visible on-chain.
  • Supply can only go down, through burning.
Why mint everything at once?

It removes “future mint surprise”: there is no function that could create more, so anyone can verify the total supply once and for all.

How release works (mechanics)
  • Only the owner can pay out, only to addresses the owner has listed, and only what the schedule has unlocked.
  • addMember() and removeMember() manage the list; release(member, amount) pays.
  • available() shows what can be paid now; unlockedAt(timestamp) shows the schedule for any date.
  • The token, the allocation and the schedule are fixed in the contract and cannot be changed.
  • Ownership cannot be renounced, and transferring it takes two steps.

3.4 Liquidity Policy

Pool liquidity comes from the Liquidity wallet and is added gradually. The Reserve wallet receives USDT from website purchases in the same transaction, and that USDT can also go into liquidity.

Reserve wallet (operations)
0x9d8dd8B2CFF7A17967fA0eF19eD5C0b3EBeFDe41
  • Receives USDT from website buys immediately.
  • Used to support liquidity and operational needs.
Liquidity approach (practical)
  • Liquidity is added as separate on-chain transactions.
  • Each operation approves the exact amounts to the router and resets the allowance to zero afterwards.
  • No permanent approvals are left on project wallets.
Website buy vs Pancake buy — what’s the difference?
  • PancakeSwap buy: standard AMM swap, price determined by pool reserves, subject to slippage.
  • Website buy: fixed price, USDT goes to the Reserve wallet, IBITI is paid from the sale contract's balance plus a bonus and an optional referral reward.
  • Both are on-chain and publicly verifiable.

4) Market & Price

There are two prices and they are independent. The website sale has a fixed price set in its contract: $0.10 per IBITI. The PancakeSwap pool has a market price set by its reserves.

The pool

The pool address is known in advance, but the pool exists only once liquidity is added. It opens at $0.10. After that the price moves with trading and can differ from the website price in either direction. The website sale does not read the pool, so trades in the pool cannot change what the website pays out.

How a PancakeSwap quote works (AMM-style) 
amountInWithFee = amountIn * (10000 - feeBps)
amountOut = (amountInWithFee * reserveOut) / (reserveIn*10000 + amountInWithFee)

This is the typical UniswapV2/PancakeV2-style quote with fee. Slippage is naturally captured.

5) Website Sale (IBITIPromoSale)

IBITIcoin website purchases are executed by a verified on-chain contract with a fixed price, a bonus and a referral reward. There is no proxy: the logic cannot be upgraded.

The sale is temporary: it can be turned off on-chain (paused, promoActive=false or promoEndTime reached), and it ends when its stock runs out.
Non-custodial USDT rule: when you buy on the website, your USDT goes directly to the Reserve Wallet in the same transaction. The sale contract never holds it. IBITI payouts come from the contract's own funded balance.

5.1 Money flow (single purchase transaction)

Buyer USDT ──────► Reserve Wallet (directly)
Sale contract IBITI ──► Buyer (purchased + 10% bonus)
Sale contract IBITI ──► Referrer (1 IBITI, once)
What this guarantees
  • USDT is never stored on the sale contract.
  • IBITI is paid only from the contract's pre-funded balance. It cannot pull tokens from any wallet.
  • All transfers are visible on-chain (token transfers per transaction hash).

5.2 What users see (MetaMask confirmations)

  • Connect wallet → choose “Promo Buy” → enter USDT amount (and optional referrer)
  • MetaMask may ask for 2 confirmations:
  • 1) USDT Approve — for the exact purchase amount, only when the allowance is insufficient. It lets the sale contract move that USDT.
  • 2) Purchase — the actual buy transaction. In this transaction USDT goes to the Reserve Wallet and IBITI is delivered (purchased amount + bonus, and referral if eligible).
  • After confirmation the website generates an IBITI Receipt (purchase check) with the tx hash and links to BscScan.

Note: the Approve is a separate transaction made by your wallet. Bonus and referral payouts are token transfers during the purchase transaction.

IBITI Receipt (purchase check) — what it contains

After a successful on-chain purchase, the website shows an IBITI Receipt (receipt modal) with a human-friendly summary and direct links to verification sources.

  • Network (Mainnet/Testnet) and timestamp
  • Buyer address and transaction hash
  • Paid (USDT)
  • Purchased amount (IBITI) + Promo bonus (IBITI) + Total received (IBITI)
  • Effective price (USDT per IBITI)
  • Links: BscScan transaction + shareable receipt link
  • Options to copy, save image, or print the receipt

Receipt values are derived from on-chain transfer logs and executed transaction data. The blockchain transaction is the ultimate source of truth.

5.3 How the contract determines your IBITI amount

The price is stored in the contract as USDT (18 decimals) per 1 whole IBITI. The buyer also passes minIbitiOut: if the purchased amount is below it, the transaction reverts. 

purchased = USDT * 10^8 / price
bonus     = purchased * bonusBps / 10000

5.4 Where your USDT goes after the buy (Reserve → Liquidity policy)

The sale contract does not add liquidity. USDT goes to the Reserve Wallet during the purchase. Liquidity is added as a separate on-chain transaction.

Step A — Guaranteed (same transaction)
  • USDT goes to the Reserve Wallet immediately.
  • The sale contract holds 0 USDT by design.
  • Anyone can verify this in token transfer logs per transaction.
Step B — Operational (separate transaction)
  • Received USDT can be used to add liquidity to the IBITI/USDT pool on PancakeSwap.
  • This happens as an additional on-chain action initiated by operations.
  • It is a policy, not an automatic on-chain guarantee.

5.5 Sale stock and accounting

The sale contract was funded with 1,000,000 IBITI by a plain transfer. A purchase that would need more than the contract holds reverts. poolRemaining() returns the real balance, and getStats() returns the bonus and referral totals.

5.7 What the owner can and cannot do

The owner can stop the sale, and while it is paused can change the price and limits and withdraw unsold IBITI. The owner can also return other tokens sent to the contract by mistake, but not IBITI that way. Ownership cannot be renounced, and transferring it takes two steps. The logic cannot be changed: a different rule means a new contract at a new address.

Transparency checklist (quick verification)
  • Open the transaction hash in BscScan and review token transfers.
  • Confirm USDT moved to the Reserve Wallet in the same transaction.
  • Confirm IBITI transfers to the buyer (and referrer if applicable).
  • Confirm the sale contract's USDT balance is 0 after the buy.

Not deployed yet. Below is the planned design. Final rules are published with the deployed contract.

Staking in IBITI is intentionally simple: choose a duration (1–12 months) → lock tokens → claim later. Rewards and penalties are known in advance.

Time rules

Reward funding

Rewards come from a separate funded reward pool, out of the marketing and reserve share. One user's stake never pays another user's reward.

8) Smart Sell Fee (Planned)

The token has no fee. Transfers and PancakeSwap trades pay no IBITI fee. The smart fee applies only to someone who chooses to sell IBITI to the project's own sell contract, which is not deployed yet.

The sell contract takes IBITI and pays USDT from its own stock. FeeManager calculates the fee for that sale, and a Discount NFT can lower it.

What makes up the fee
  • Base sell fee: 10%
  • Active stake: fee × 0.9
  • VIP status: −2 points
  • Whale-size sale: +3 points
  • Discount NFT: lowers the result
What reviewers care about
  • Fee clamped by min/max bounds
  • Admin setters visible on-chain
  • UserStatusManager holds VIP and whale flags only; it cannot freeze or block tokens
  • The token does not read any of these contracts
Reality check — fees are a policy tool, not a miracle

Final values are set at deployment. Fees can support sustainability and discourage abuse, but they do not remove market risk.

9) Buyback (Deferred)

BuybackManager is not deployed. If it is used later, it would acquire IBITI on the market and optionally burn it. This is an operational tool — not a guaranteed promise.

Key idea

If buybacks are used, they should be executed with slippage protections and transparent reporting. Burn is verifiable on-chain.

10) Security & Admin Controls

Who controls what
  • Token: nobody. It has no owner.
  • Vesting: the owner pays out, only within the schedule.
  • Website sale: the owner has the powers in section 5.7.
  • No live contract is behind a proxy, so none can be upgraded.
Protections in place
  • Source code verified on BscScan and public on GitHub
  • OpenZeppelin base contracts, reentrancy guard on purchases
  • Price bounds and decimals checks in the sale contract
  • Mainnet owner wallets are hardware wallets
Trust model (honest version)

The website sale has an owner who can change its price and limits while it is paused and withdraw its unsold stock. This is a trust assumption. The token, by contrast, has no owner: nobody can stop it, freeze an address or reverse a transfer, including in an emergency.

11) Transparency & Monitoring

What anyone can verify

  • Website buys: by watching the contract events (PromoBuy).
  • USDT routing: the sale contract holds 0 USDT; the Reserve wallet receives it.
  • Sale stock: poolRemaining() returns the real balance.
  • Vesting: available(), released() and Released events.

12) Risks & Limitations (Read This)

No hype. DeFi is risky. Liquidity can be thin. Smart contracts can fail. This section exists so reviewers and users don’t have to guess.

Market & liquidity risk

  • Until the pool has liquidity, there is no open market to sell IBITI.
  • Low liquidity increases slippage and price volatility.
  • The market price can fall below the website price.

Smart contract risk

  • The contracts have tests and verified source code, but no independent third-party audit yet.
  • Contracts cannot be upgraded, so a bug can only be addressed by a new deployment.

Admin & wallet risk

  • The website sale's parameters can change while it is paused, as described in section 5.7.
  • The token cannot be paused or frozen, so stolen tokens cannot be recovered by the project.

Planned modules

  • Staking, NFTs and the sell contract are not live. Their rules can change before deployment.

13) FAQ

Why have a website buy flow if Pancake exists?

Pancake is the open market. The website flow is a fixed-price on-chain sale with a bonus, a referral reward and strict limits, designed for simple UX and promotions.

Where do my USDT go after buying on the website?

Your USDT goes to the Reserve Wallet in the same transaction. The sale contract never holds it.

After the USDT reaches the Reserve Wallet, it can be used for liquidity operations (e.g., adding liquidity to the IBITI/USDT pool on PancakeSwap). This step is a separate on-chain transaction and is fully traceable.

Receipt: after a successful purchase the website shows an IBITI Receipt (a “check”) with transaction hash, paid USDT, received IBITI (purchased + bonus), effective price, and direct links to BscScan.

Does the token take a fee on transfers or trades?

No. The token has no fee at all. On PancakeSwap you pay only the pool's own swap fee and gas.

Can more IBITI be created?

No. The token has no mint function and no owner. Supply can only go down, through burning.

Are Discount NFTs reusable?

In the planned design, most discount NFTs are single-use (use → burn). Pandora is special: it does not expire but has a usage limit (10 per 360 days).
